Appointment Cancellation Policy

Guidelines for rescheduling, cancellations, and clinic courtesy

1. Our Commitment to Dedicated Specialist Time

At Enamel Microscopic Dentistry, our appointments are scheduled for generous, unhurried treatment windows—often 45 to 90 minutes dedicated exclusively to one patient under the operating microscope. When an appointment is missed or cancelled without adequate notice, that dedicated specialist time cannot be offered to other patients requiring urgent dental care.

2. Advance Notice for Cancellation & Rescheduling

  • Standard Consultations & Routine Procedures: We kindly request at least 4 to 6 hours advance notice if you need to reschedule or cancel your visit.
  • Extended Microscopic Procedures & Surgeries (60+ Minutes): For multi-hour surgical appointments, implant placements, or smile makeover deliveries, we appreciate at least 24 hours advance notice.

3. How to Reschedule or Cancel

You can conveniently cancel or reschedule your reserved appointment by:

  • Calling our clinic desk directly at +91 88673 74949.
  • Sending a WhatsApp message quoting your unique Booking Reference (e.g. EMD-2026-XXXX).
  • Replying to your appointment confirmation email or SMS.

4. Emergency Situations

We recognize that unforeseen personal, medical, or travel emergencies occur. In such cases, please notify us as early as feasible so our team can accommodate your next visit seamlessly.

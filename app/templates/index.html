CLINICAL PRECISION BENCHMARK

Why We Perform Dentistry Under A Microscope

The human eye can resolve details down to roughly 0.2 millimeters. In contrast, dental nerve canals and structural enamel micro-fractures measure mere microns. Operating without high magnification means relying on tactile estimation.

At Enamel, high-powered surgical microscopes provide up to 2500% greater visual clarity, allowing our doctors to detect hidden root canals, seal restorative margins seamlessly, and save teeth that would otherwise be deemed unsalvageable.

✓
Hidden Canal Detection: Finds accessory canals (e.g., MB2 in molars) often missed by conventional unmagnified endodontics.
✓
Pericervical Dentin Preservation: Prevents excessive tooth drilling, ensuring your natural tooth remains structurally resilient for decades.
✓
Aesthetic Margin Blending: Allows microscopic composite layering and veneer bonding that mimics natural enamel light transmission.

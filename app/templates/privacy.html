Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 • Patient Confidentiality Standard

1. Patient Confidentiality & Information Collection

At Enamel Microscopic Dentistry ("Enamel", "the Clinic"), protecting your personal and medical information is a core clinical obligation. When you book an appointment, submit an enquiry, or receive treatment at our clinic in Mysuru, Karnataka, we collect necessary contact information (name, phone number, email address) and clinical history to ensure safe, tailored dental care.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your details are used exclusively for:

  • Scheduling, confirming, rescheduling, and reminding you of your dental appointments.
  • Maintaining confidential clinical records and radiographic diagnostic archives.
  • Communicating treatment plans, pre-operative instructions, and post-operative care advisories.
  • Responding to your specific dental enquiries submitted through our website or WhatsApp.

We do not sell, rent, or trade patient personal data to third-party commercial marketing entities under any circumstances.

3. Medical Imaging & Dental Photographs

Diagnostic photographs and microscope video captures obtained during clinical treatment are part of your confidential medical record. Any educational use or anonymized smile case documentation is undertaken only with explicit patient written consent.

4. Security of Data

Our backend database incorporates strict access controls, encrypted transport layers (HTTPS), and secure administrative credential hashing. Only authorized clinic staff have access to appointment and health scheduling information.

5. Contact Regarding Your Data

If you have questions regarding your data or wish to update your records, please contact our clinic at contact@enamelmd.com or call +91 88673 74949.
